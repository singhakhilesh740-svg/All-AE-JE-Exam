Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how All AE/JE Civil Exams ("we", "our", "the App"), operated by Akhilesh Singh, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website (www.allaejecivilexams.in) and Android application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with your Google account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Google profile picture (if available)
  • A unique Google user ID

You may also optionally provide:

  • Your state of residence
  • The exam you are preparing for

We also automatically collect basic usage data such as which sections of the app you visit, quiz attempts, bookmarks, and reported issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in
  • To save your bookmarks, quiz history, and preferences across devices
  • To improve the app and fix bugs based on reports you send
  • To communicate important updates about the app

3. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage). Firebase is operated by Google LLC and complies with industry-standard security practices.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with:

  • Google Firebase — for authentication and data storage
  • Law enforcement — only if required by valid legal request

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for users aged 18 and above (exam aspirants). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • View your profile information within the app
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us
  • Sign out at any time from the app

7. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon deletion request, we will remove your data within 30 days.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption and Firebase security rules to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
